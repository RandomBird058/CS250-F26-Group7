Group Agreement

How work will be divided:
Work will be divided by tasks. Each person will agree to complete a task and is the main worker for that task. But, if help is needed, they can ask for assistance.

How group members will communicate:
We will communicate primarily on Discord and through GitHub projects.

Expected response times:
Expect a response within 24 hours.

How deadlines will be handled:
Deadlines will be set when tasks are created.

What happens if someone cannot complete their assigned work:
If someone cannot complete their work, let everyone know BEFORE the deadline so that others can assist. This ensures that the work gets done even if one person can't do it.

How disagreements will be resolved:
If there is a disagreement between members, the outcome will be decided by a vote. At the moment, we have 4 people to a 3-1 vote will need to be achieved.

When/how you will meet outside of class if necessary:
We can meet either online on Discord or in the library on campus at a scheduled time.

Who will be responsible for final submissions:
The repo owner (Dominic) is most likely responsible but anyone can submit.
